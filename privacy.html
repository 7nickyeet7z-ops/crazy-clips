Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-02  |  ← Back to Crazy Clips

1. Who we are

Crazy Clips is a video automation platform that helps gaming content creators generate and upload short-form clips to their TikTok accounts. Contact: 7nickyeet7z@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access/refresh tokens) — stored locally on the creator's machine only.
  • Basic profile info (open ID, display name) — obtained via user.info.basic scope to confirm the authenticated account.
  • Local processing files — clips, subtitles, captions, and logs created during video processing.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate with TikTok on behalf of the creator via OAuth.
  • To upload video drafts to the creator's TikTok Inbox using the video.upload scope.
  • To maintain a local processing log and avoid re-processing completed work.

4. Data sharing

Crazy Clips does not sell or share personal information with any third parties, except that uploaded media is transmitted to TikTok solely to place drafts into the creator's Inbox.

5. Storage and security

All tokens and logs are stored locally on the creator's device. The creator is responsible for device security and access control. No data is stored on external servers by Crazy Clips.

6. Data retention

Processed videos and logs remain on the creator's device until archived or deleted by the creator. Authorization tokens are stored until the creator revokes access or deletes the local configuration.

7. Your rights

Creators can revoke Crazy Clips' access to their TikTok account at any time via TikTok's app settings under "Manage app permissions".

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: 7nickyeet7z@gmail.com
